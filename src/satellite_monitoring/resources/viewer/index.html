Method, quality rules, and provenance

Green and short-wave infrared reflectance give MNDWI = (green − SWIR) / (green + SWIR). We compare only pixels valid on both dates. SCL classes 4, 5, and 6 are retained; nodata, cloud, cirrus, shadow, snow, and unclassified pixels are excluded. Area uses the projected grid's pixel area.

These original 2020 products have scale 0.0001 and offset 0. Reprojection aligns both dates to one 20 m grid. The base images are actual Sentinel true-color pixels. Coordinates are in the stated UTM grid; the downloaded footprint supplies WGS84 geography.

This dry-season/monsoon comparison includes normal seasonal change. NASA's historical account supplies regional context, not labels or accuracy validation for this crop. This is a retrospective analysis, with no live feed or warning function.
